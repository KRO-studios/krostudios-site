The laugh lands in three beats.

No account, game server, or pile of phones. Gather around one device and start a round.

01 · SAY IT

Record the secret dare

The Maker reads a phrase or performs a sound. Echo Dare reverses the clip directly on the device.

02 · ECHO IT

Copy the backwards sound

Pass the phone. The next player hears only the strange reversed version and imitates it as closely as possible.

03 · REVEAL IT

Flip it and compare

Hear both voices, reveal the Echo Match, react as a room, and crown the party's best accidental linguist. Echo Match is a playful comparison of timing, sound shape, and pauses; it does not recognize words or meaning.

Built for the room

Tiny setup. Loud payoff.

Make it personal

Add session-only names and inside jokes, or choose Mix It Up, Say It Silly, or Sound Chaos.

Everyone gets a turn

Fair rotating roles, fast rematches, party awards, room reactions, and a local leaderboard.

Share only when you choose

Create a winner card or explicitly share a decoded clip through your phone's normal share sheet.

Your voices stay in the room.

Voice clips are processed on your device and are not uploaded to an Echo Dare server. A clip leaves the App only if you choose to share it. Core gameplay works offline; internet access may be used for advertising and consent messages.
